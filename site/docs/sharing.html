4 · Sharing

Share with control.

Hand anything in your inbox to another device or person — without giving away your workspace. Every public link is expiring, countable, and revocable.

Public share links

From any item, create a public handoff at /s/:token — a page (plus unlock and file endpoints) that works without a login. From the dashboard UI or the CLI:

9t share ab12cd34 --lifetime 1d --password optional-password
ControlHow it works
ExpiryEvery share has a lifetime (e.g. 1d). Expired objects are purged by the hourly cleanup sweep — deletion and expiry converge on one lifecycle.
PasswordOptional password on any share. Tokens are signed and expiring; access without the password (when set) is rejected.
Access countsEach share tracks how often it was opened. List shares with GET /api/shares or in the dashboard.
RevokeDelete the share (DELETE /api/shares/:id) at any time. The link stops working immediately; the underlying object stays in your workspace.

Design rule: a 9t instance is never reachable without authentication — shares are the only public surface, and each one is scoped to a single object with a signed, expiring token.

QR handoff to phones

  1. Open the item's stable page (/o/xxxx — 4-letter IDs are made to be read out and typed).
  2. Show its QR code on screen.
  3. Scan it with the phone: on the same LAN it opens the local address; shared publicly it opens the expiring /s/:token link.

QR handoff pairs well with the Android client: scan, receive into Downloads/9t, done. For a permanent phone connection instead of a one-off link, see pairing.
